# Sipimals Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

> Before public release, add the publisher's legal name and real support contact, identify the policy hosting provider and how it handles page visits, publish this page at a public HTTPS URL, and use that URL in App Store Connect.

## Information stored by the app

Sipimals stores drink type, amount, and time; your daily goal; your selected animal; and earned animal unlocks. The app keeps these details in its private app storage on your device so it can show your progress and history.

The app does not require an account. In the current release, it has no developer-operated server or cloud sync and includes no analytics or advertising SDKs. Your app records are not sent to the app publisher.

If you choose to open a research link in Settings, iOS opens that third-party website. The website then handles your visit under its own privacy policy.

## How the information is used

The app uses your drink entries to show daily totals, history, goal credit, streak progress, and animal unlocks. The daily goal and companion preference personalize the app on your device.

## Retention and deletion

Your entries and preferences remain in the app's local storage until you delete entries or remove the app. You can delete individual drink entries from Today or History. There is no account or developer-side copy to delete. Earned animal unlocks are kept in app preferences until the app is removed; the app currently has no separate reset control for those unlocks.

If you enable device backups, iOS may include app data in a backup. Backup settings and backup retention are controlled by Apple and your device settings.

## Privacy requests and contact

This app does not collect records on a developer-operated service, so it has no server-side drink history or account to access, export, or delete. For help or privacy questions, contact the publisher using the support contact that will be published with this app.

Before publication, identify the provider hosting this policy and link to its privacy terms if it processes page visit or connection data.

## Changes

If the app later adds an account, sync, analytics, advertising, or another data flow, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosures must be updated before that feature is released.
